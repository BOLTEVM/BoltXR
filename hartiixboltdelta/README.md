## 2. Design Synopsis

The piece fuses the pure analog soul of 1960s-1970s motorsport racing watches with cutting-edge decentralized crypto infrastructure:
1. **Case:** 38.5mm cushion / C-shape 316L stainless steel case with satin-brushed top surfaces and high-polished 45-degree chamfered bevels.
2. **Crystal:** Box-dome anti-reflective sapphire crystal offering vintage edge distortion.
3. **Dial:** Warm champagne/eggshell granular sector dial with crosshairs and applied patinated brass hour batons.
4. **Hands:** Heat-blued syringe hands and blued needle second hand.
5. **Complication (The Hartii Block Window):** Amber phosphor dot-matrix digital display at 6 o'clock broadcasting real-time blockchain telemetry:
   * `BLOCK 10,812,345`
   * `DIFF 1.24 T`
6. **Strap:** Distressed cognac perforated Italian rally leather with off-white saddle stitching.
7. **Security & Hardware:** EAL6+ secure enclave, biometric pulse sensor in titanium caseback, kinetic micro-piezoelectric supercapacitor.
